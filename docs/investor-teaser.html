Problem

Medical bills are opaque by design — coded, error-prone, and paired with a separate insurer EOB no patient can reconcile. Disputing means hours on the phone and deadlines that quietly expire.

Solution

Scan → understand → act, privately on-device. BillGuard explains the codes, benchmarks the price, reconciles the EOB, and produces the specific next action: a question, a correction, or an appeal.
