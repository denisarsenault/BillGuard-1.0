Fundraising memorandum

Financing the fight against medical bills.

Roughly 100 million Americans carry medical debt, and most bills are opaque by design. BillGuard turns a phone camera into the tool that reads the bill, catches the error, and gets money back. We're raising a pre-seed angel round to convert a working product into a repeatable business.

01

Why now

Three forces made this buildable and fundable in 2026, not 2016.

Regulation

Transparency is law

The No Surprises Act (2022) and hospital price-transparency rules created both consumer awareness and machine-readable price data to benchmark against — the fuel BillGuard runs on.

Technology

On-device AI matured

Vision-framework OCR and small language models now parse a messy bill on the phone, privately, at near-zero marginal cost. That wasn't true a few years ago.

Sentiment

Trust collapsed

Distrust of hospital billing is at a peak; "fight your medical bill" is a cultural genre. Demand is pulling, not being pushed.

A patient receives a bill written in codes, a separate EOB from the insurer, and no way to tell whether the two agree or whether the charge is fair. Disputing means hours on the phone, deadlines that quietly expire, and jargon designed to make people give up and pay. That friction is the moat incumbents rely on — and the opportunity BillGuard attacks.

03

Solution & moat

Read → understand → act, on-device and private — with defensibility that compounds.

BillGuard scans any medical bill, extracts the line items and codes, explains them in plain English with fair-price context, reconciles them against the insurer's EOB, and produces the specific next action — a question, a correction, or an appeal. It runs on the user's own devices via iCloud, so sensitive data never centralizes.

Moat 01

Proprietary code + price layer

A structured CPT/HCPCS/ICD dataset with plain-English explanations and price bands, extendable with transparency-file data. It powers both the product and an SEO surface competitors can't cheaply replicate.

Moat 02

Privacy as positioning

On-device processing is a genuine trust differentiator in a category built on distrust — and it lowers infrastructure cost and breach surface.

Moat 03

Multi-surface capture

Phone camera, file/PDF import, and a browser extension that captures bills inside patient portals — meeting bills wherever they live.

Moat 04

Outcome data flywheel

Every reconciled bill and won appeal sharpens error detection and price accuracy — a data asset that grows with usage.

05

Business model & unit economics

Multiple lines, layered as the product earns the right to each.

Line 01 · now

Consumer subscription

Freemium: scanning & explanations free; error detection, EOB reconciliation, appeals and price benchmarks behind a subscription (est. ~$5–10/mo or ~$40–60/yr).

Line 02 · mid

Success fees

Optional managed negotiation — a percentage of dollars actually saved. Aligns revenue with outcomes and needs no ad budget to convert.

Line 03 · later

B2B2C & API

White-label for employers, plans and TPAs; license the code/price API. Higher ACV, longer sales, real durability.

The economics that matter
  • Near-zero marginal cost — on-device processing means each user is cheap to serve.
  • CAC starts low — organic SEO/content/community before any paid spend.
  • LTV rises with lines — subscription + success fees + eventual B2B compound per relationship.
  • The number to prove — a free user reliably becoming a paying one. Everything else follows.

10

Finding & closing angels

Where they are, what they want, and the traps that kill first raises.

Where to find them

Best odds

Warm intros

Map your network for anyone touching health, fintech or consumer apps; ask specifically for intros to angels who invest there. A warm intro beats a hundred cold emails.

Operator angels

People who lived this

Founders/execs from health-fintech, insurance, or billing who exited. They write checks and open doors, and they get the problem instantly.

Platforms

Wellfound & syndicates

Wellfound (AngelList) for angels, and syndicates that let one lead pull a group. Consumer-health and fintech syndicates exist specifically for this.

Programs

Accelerators

Y Combinator / Techstars for signal + network; healthcare-specific programs (Redesign Health, Cedars-Sinai, MATTER, Rock Health community) for domain depth.

Groups & events

Angel networks & pitch nights

Regional and healthcare-focused angel groups, plus demo days and pitch events for reps and warm leads.

The crowd

Equity crowdfunding

Wefunder / Republic turn early users into investors — uniquely powerful for a consumer app where backers become evangelists.

What they're actually buying

  • Founder–market fit — why you, why this, why now.
  • Pull, not push — early users who came unbidden and stayed.
  • A wedge with a path — small entry, credibly large outcome.
  • Clean cap table — no messy prior deals or dead equity.
  • Honest metrics — retention and willingness-to-pay you can defend.
Run the process like a funnel

Build a target list of 40–60 fitting angels. Open with the strongest warm intros, create a light data room (this memo, a demo, a metrics one-pager), and run meetings in tight batches to manufacture momentum. Fundraising is a sales pipeline — treat it like one.

Traps that kill first raises

Timing

Raising before there's pull

Pre-traction, you sell a story on worst-case terms. Bootstrap to the first proof points in the Launch Plan, then raise from strength.

Sizing

Too little to reach the next round

Under-raising strands you between milestones. Size the round to clear the seed bar with a margin of safety.

Diligence

Sensitive data & regulatory hand-waving

Investors will probe privacy, HIPAA posture, and the accuracy/liability of price and error claims. Have crisp, honest answers — vagueness reads as risk.

Fit

Wrong money

A check with no network or domain help costs more than it's worth. Optimize for investors who accelerate you, not just the highest cap.
