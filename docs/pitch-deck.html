03 / 12Why now

Three forces made this buildable in 2026.

Law

Transparency is mandated

The No Surprises Act and price-transparency rules created awareness — and machine-readable price data to benchmark against.

Technology

On-device AI matured

Vision OCR and small models now parse a messy bill on the phone, privately, at near-zero marginal cost.

Sentiment

Trust collapsed

"Fight your medical bill" is now a cultural genre. Demand is pulling, not being pushed.

04 / 12The solution

Scan. Understand. Act.

01

Scan

Snap a bill or import a PDF. On-device OCR extracts every line item and code.

02

Understand

Plain-English explanations, fair-price context, and reconciliation against your insurer's EOB.

03

Act

Flag the overcharge and generate the specific next step — a question, a correction, or an appeal.

05 / 12Product & moat

Private by design. Defensible by data.

Moat 01

Code + price dataset

Structured CPT/HCPCS/ICD data with plain-English text and price bands — product engine and SEO surface in one.

Moat 02

On-device privacy

Bills never centralize. Genuine trust differentiator, lower cost, smaller breach surface.

Moat 03

Capture anywhere

Phone camera, PDF import, and a browser extension that grabs bills inside patient portals.

07 / 12Business model

Three lines, layered over time.

Now

Consumer subscription

Freemium: scanning free; error detection, EOB reconciliation, appeals & benchmarks paid.

Mid

Success fees

A share of dollars actually saved — revenue aligned with outcomes.

Later

B2B2C & API

White-label for employers & plans; license the code/price API. Higher ACV, real durability.

08 / 12Competition

Nobody owns the private, consumer-first wedge.

Negotiation services

Expensive, high-friction, done-for-you. We're self-serve first, managed later.

Price-data sites

Data, but no action on your bill. We apply price data to the actual bill.

Benefits navigators

Locked to an employer, generic. We work for anyone, direct.

Generic scanners / PFM

No billing intelligence. We're purpose-built for the bill.
